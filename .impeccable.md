## Design Context

### Users
Personal tool — a single person, used daily as a quick emotional check-in and private journal. The context is intimate: opening the app at the end of a long day, a quiet moment between meetings, or whenever a feeling needs to be named. Speed matters — the log flow should feel frictionless.

### Brand Personality
**Alive · Warm · Expressive**

Not corporate wellness. Not clinical. More like a personal sketchbook with a pulse. The 8-mood color system *is* the identity — each mood has its own color, its own weight. Emotions are valid and colorful, not to be managed or suppressed.

### Aesthetic Direction
Playful-warm with a personal feel. References: Streaks, Finch — apps that celebrate small wins and feel like they're rooting for you. Anti-reference: generic SaaS wellness dashboards, clinical white interfaces, flat productivity apps.

- **Color**: Bold, emoji-forward. The mood palette (warm gradient for CTA, per-mood colors for data) should feel alive — never muted into oblivion.
- **Typography**: System stack is fine for this personal PWA. Tight tracking on display elements, generous line-height for journal notes.
- **Motion**: GSAP animations are the heartbeat. Should feel snappy and alive, not performative.
- **Dark mode default**: Feels like a late-night journal. Light mode for daytime use.

### Design Principles
1. **Emotion is the content** — Every color, every animation exists to reflect the user's emotional state. Design serves the mood system, not the other way around.
2. **Fast in, fast out** — The log flow must be completable in under 10 seconds. No friction, no cognitive load.
3. **Celebrate every log** — Small wins matter. Streaks, success animations, warm empty states. The app should feel like it's genuinely happy when you show up.
4. **Personal, not product** — Intimate over polished-corporate. Details that feel handcrafted and intentional, not templated.
5. **Colors are living things** — The per-mood colors aren't decoration; they're data. Never gray them out when they should be communicating.
